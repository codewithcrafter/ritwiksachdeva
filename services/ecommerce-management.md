### What does an e-commerce management service include?

E-commerce management can cover product cataloging and SKU mapping, PPC and advertising management, inventory forecasting and stock synchronization, account health monitoring, order fulfillment coordination, and ongoing store or marketplace maintenance. The exact scope depends on the platforms involved and your business requirements.

### Which e-commerce platforms do you manage?

Management covers Amazon Seller Central, Flipkart Seller Hub, and Shopify stores primarily, along with multi-channel coordination for Meesho, Myntra, and Quick Commerce platforms like Blinkit, Zepto, and Instamart.

### Do you provide Amazon and Flipkart account management?

Yes. This includes end-to-end account management across both platforms: listing and catalog optimization, PPC campaigns, Buy Box monitoring, A+ content, FBA/Smart-FA fulfillment coordination, and account health maintenance.

### Do you manage Amazon PPC and Sponsored Products?

Yes. Amazon PPC management covers Sponsored Products, Sponsored Brands, and Sponsored Display ads, focused on improving Return on Ad Spend (ROAS) and reducing wasted spend on non-converting search terms.

### Can you help with Amazon account suspension and Plan of Action (POA)?

Yes. We support sellers with account reinstatement by identifying the root cause of the suspension or policy violation and drafting structured Plans of Action (POAs) aligned with platform guidelines.

### Do you provide Shopify store management?

Yes. Shopify management covers catalog and inventory updates, app and payment gateway integrations, Meta and Google Ads pixel setup, and ongoing store optimization for speed and conversion.

### Can you integrate Shopify with Indian payment and logistics providers?

Yes. We configure standard Indian payment gateways (Razorpay, Cashfree, PayU, PhonePe) and shipping integrations (Shiprocket, Delhivery, NimbusPost) to support domestic D2C operations.

### How can e-commerce management reduce RTO and DTO?

RTO reduction starts with accurate catalog details, clear product specifications, size guides, honest imagery, and identifying return patterns from customer feedback to fix the root cause before orders ship.

### Do you manage inventory and stock synchronization across multiple marketplaces?

Yes. Multi-channel stock coordination helps prevent overselling and out-of-stock penalties across Amazon, Flipkart, and Shopify through regular inventory forecasting and sync workflows.

### Is e-commerce management better than hiring an in-house manager?

It depends on your scale. Outsourcing provides immediate access to specialized skills across advertising, cataloging, and policy without full-time payroll overhead. An in-house hire makes sense once order volume requires a dedicated full-time presence daily.

### How much do e-commerce management services cost in Delhi?

Pricing is typically structured as a monthly retainer, a percentage of GMV, or a hybrid model, depending on monthly revenue, catalog size, and whether PPC advertising management is included in the scope.

### Why choose an e-commerce management partner with digital marketing and PPC experience?

An e-commerce account cannot grow through operations alone  it needs profitable advertising and conversion optimization. A partner with digital marketing and PPC expertise ensures your listings are built to convert and your ad spend delivers actual ROAS.
