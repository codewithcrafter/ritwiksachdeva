Yes. Every project includes dynamic on-screen subtitles, curated B-roll footage, sound effects, licensed background music, and motion graphic title cards.

### How long does video editing take?

Short-form Reels and Shorts are delivered within 24 to 48 hours. Long-form YouTube videos, podcasts, and corporate edits typically take 3 to 5 business days.

### How much do video editing services cost in Delhi?

Costs depend on video duration, raw footage quality, motion graphic complexity, and project volume. We offer both single-video project rates and discounted monthly retainer bundles.

### Is it better to hire a freelance video editor or a video editing agency?

### Do you provide e-commerce product video editing?

Yes. We edit product showcase videos, unboxing clips, and lifestyle feature demonstrations designed to increase conversion rates on Shopify and marketplace listings.

### How do you make video editing focused on engagement and retention?

By analyzing retention graphs, structuring the first 3 seconds with pattern-interrupt hooks, cutting dead air, using subtle zooms to maintain visual momentum, and aligning cuts to audio beats.
