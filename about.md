## Detailed Core Sections & Service Architecture

### How This Started

Ritwik’s interest in marketing didn’t begin with a course — it began with watching businesses spend on ads and content without a clear read on what was actually converting. That gap between “activity” and “results” is what pulled him toward performance-driven marketing.

### How He Works Today

Every engagement starts with an audit, not a pitch — understanding what’s already working, what’s leaking budget, and where the biggest gap is between effort and outcome.

From there, the plan is built around the client’s actual constraints: budget, team size, timeline — not a one-size-fits-all package. Reporting stays transparent throughout, so clients always know what’s being spent and what it’s producing.
