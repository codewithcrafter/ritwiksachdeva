Frequently Asked Questions About Marketing Analytics

Technical implementation details and server requirements explained.

Standard client-side GTM runs in the user's browser, where it is increasingly blocked by ad-blockers, Safari's Intelligent Tracking Prevention (ITP), and privacy settings. Server-Side GTM runs on your own first-party cloud domain. It restores complete signal integrity, extends cookie lifespan from 7 to 365 days, protects sensitive user data, and speeds up your site by reducing browser JavaScript overhead.

Server-side GTM runs on Google Cloud Platform (GCP) or specialized providers like Stape.io. For most mid-market companies with under 500,000 monthly page views, server hosting costs are negligible—typically between $20 and $50 per month—a tiny fraction of the thousands saved in optimized ad spend.
