Content Pitfalls

Why 95% of Corporate Content Fails to Generate Commercial Influence

The internet is overflowing with low-effort, AI-generated rehashes of existing articles. C-level executives don't read "Top 5 Marketing Tips"—they read data-backed original insights from practitioners who understand their business reality.

Shallow, Commodity Articles

Hiring cheap freelance copywriters who write surface-level listicles that damage your executive brand credibility and fail to educate buying committees.

The "Post & Pray" Distribution Void

Companies spend 40 hours producing a whitepaper, post it on their blog, tweet a link once, and watch it die with 12 total views because they have no systematic distribution engine.

Rented Audience Fragility

Relying entirely on LinkedIn or Google algorithms means a single platform policy change can wipe out 80% of your reach overnight. You must build an owned subscriber moat.
