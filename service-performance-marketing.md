## Frequently Asked Questions (Comprehensive Q&A)

### How do I choose the best performance marketing consultant for my D2C brand in Delhi NCR?

Look for a consultant who begins with an audit of your tracking and attribution infrastructure (GA4, CAPI, GTM) before proposing media spend, and who measures success by Blended ROAS, CAC, and unit economics rather than superficial platform metrics.

### What does a performance marketing consultant charge per month in Delhi?

Consulting retainers typically range from a flat monthly fee to a percentage of managed ad spend (typically 10-15%) or a hybrid base plus performance bonus. The structure depends on current monthly spend and technical scope.

### What is the difference between a digital marketing agency and a performance marketing consultant?

A digital marketing agency often offers broad generalist services (social posts, PR, branding) handled by junior staff. A performance marketing consultant brings focused, senior-level specialization in paid media algorithms, tracking, CRO, and unit economics.

### Can a performance marketing consultant help lower customer acquisition costs (CAC) for my e-commerce business?

Yes. CAC reduction is achieved by pruning wasteful broad search queries, deploying Meta CAPI to feed machine learning clean data, building high-retention creative testing funnels, and optimizing landing page conversion rates.

### What platforms do performance marketing consultants focus on in India?

The core performance channels in India are Meta Ads Manager (Instagram/Facebook) and Google Ads (Search, Shopping, PMax, YouTube), supplemented by LinkedIn for B2B and WhatsApp Business API for direct conversion and retention.

### What is the average minimum ad budget required to hire a performance marketing consultant in Delhi?

While there is no strict barrier, businesses typically benefit most when allocating at least ₹50,000 to ₹1,50,000+ in monthly ad spend, which provides sufficient data volume for smart bidding algorithms to optimize.

### Do performance marketing consultants handle creative ad design and copywriting?

Yes. Creative strategy is the single largest lever in modern paid media. We provide direct-response copywriting, visual hook frameworks, static graphic ads, and video creative briefs tailored for feed conversion.

### How do performance marketing agencies report ROI to clients?

Reporting is delivered via real-time Looker Studio dashboards tracking first-party metrics: Blended ROAS, Marketing Efficiency Ratio (MER), Net Revenue, Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC), and channel-specific CPA.

### What is a good ROAS benchmark for e-commerce brands in India?

Healthy ROAS depends on gross product margins. For fashion and lifestyle brands, a first-order ROAS of 2.8x to 4.5x is standard, while higher-margin categories aim for 4.5x to 6x+ blended across paid channels.

### How does ROI-driven performance marketing reduce wasted ad spend?

By establishing negative keyword lists, excluding non-converting placements, disabling audience expansion bleed, and auditing tracking pixels so algorithms never bid on accidental clicks or bot traffic.
