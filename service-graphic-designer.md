### How much does a freelance graphic designer charge in Delhi NCR?

Pricing depends heavily on scope  a simple logo or social media package is priced very differently from a full brand identity system with a style guide, collateral, and packaging design. Contact us with your requirements for a clear, itemized proposal.

### What is the difference between a graphic designer and a branding design consultant?

A graphic designer typically executes individual assets (a flyer, a banner, a post). A branding design consultant works at the strategic level first: defining positioning, target audience visual psychology, color theory, typography hierarchy, and a master design language so every asset feels like part of one cohesive brand.

### What is included in a complete brand identity design package?

A complete identity package typically includes the master logo mark, secondary variations, monochrome lockups, primary and secondary color palettes (HEX, RGB, CMYK, Pantone), font pairings and hierarchy, graphic motifs, a brand guidelines book, and essential collateral (business cards, stationery, social media brand kit).

### How do I hire the best freelance branding consultant in Delhi?

Look for someone who begins by understanding your business model, customer psychology, and competitive landscape before jumping into sketches. Also look for cross-disciplinary expertise in digital marketing so your visuals translate seamlessly to high-converting websites and ads.

### Why hire a freelance graphic designer instead of a design agency?

Working directly with a freelance consultant means zero account manager markups, direct communication with the person crafting the visuals, faster turnaround sprints, and consistent creative vision without junior agency delegate handoffs.

### Do you design social media creatives and ad creatives, not just logos?

### Can you help with a company profile or pitch deck, not just a logo?

### Do you offer rebranding services for businesses that already have a logo?

Yes. Rebranding engagements analyze what brand equity to preserve while modernizing typography, color systems, and collateral architecture to reposition your company for higher-value clients.

### Is packaging and label design part of your branding services?

Yes. We develop production-ready packaging die-lines, labels, box sleeves, and retail packaging compliant with Indian legal standards and optimized for retail shelf and e-commerce thumbnail appeal.
