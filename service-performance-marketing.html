Why Most Paid Advertising Budgets Fail to Scale Profitably

Post-iOS14 privacy restrictions and saturated auction environments have exposed lazy ad tactics. Without mathematical discipline and attribution hygiene, paid acquisition quickly becomes an unsustainable money pit.

Ad agencies celebrate cheap clicks, impressions, and low Cost-Per-Click (CPC) numbers. But your sales team is drowning in junk leads that never turn into closed-won customers.
