About Ritwik Sachdeva

The Person Behind the Strategy

Most digital marketing pages talk about services. This page is about the person planning them — how Ritwik got into digital marketing, the way he actually works with clients, and why his approach leans on data over guesswork.

Based in Delhi, Ritwik works closely with founders and marketing teams — not through templated packages, but through strategies built around each business’s specific numbers, audience, and goals.

How This Started

Ritwik’s interest in marketing didn’t begin with a course — it began with watching businesses spend on ads and content without a clear read on what was actually converting. That gap between “activity” and “results” is what pulled him toward performance-driven marketing.

Over time, that grew into a full consulting practice covering SEO, paid campaigns, social media, and web presence — always tied back to one question: is this generating leads or revenue the client can point to?

Working Approach

How He Works Today

Every engagement starts with an audit, not a pitch — understanding what’s already working, what’s leaking budget, and where the biggest gap is between effort and outcome.

From there, the plan is built around the client’s actual constraints: budget, team size, timeline — not a one-size-fits-all package. Reporting stays transparent throughout, so clients always know what’s being spent and what it’s producing.
