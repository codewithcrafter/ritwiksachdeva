Consultant Background

A Decade Dedicated to Marketing Performance.

My career began over a decade ago in the analytical side of direct-response marketing and search engine algorithms. I observed early on that many businesses struggle not from product shortcomings, but because their marketing channels are fragmented, unmeasured, and inefficient.

Over the years, I managed multi-channel media budgets, audited complex conversion funnels, and engineered organic search architectures for companies across North America, Europe, and Asia. From early-stage B2B SaaS platforms to established service enterprises, my core conviction remains clear:

"Marketing should never be treated as an unmeasured overhead expense. When structured properly around unit economics, it functions as a predictable, high-yield growth asset."

Today, I operate independently as a senior digital marketing consultant. This model allows me to provide hands-on strategic counsel directly to decision-makers—eliminating the agency bureaucracy that often dilutes strategic execution.
