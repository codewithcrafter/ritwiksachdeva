Platform Specs

Formats & Platforms Covered

All edits are optimized and exported in native platform aspect ratios and compression codecs: 9:16 vertical for Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, and TikTok; 16:9 widescreen for YouTube longform, websites, and corporate presentations; and 1:1 or 4:5 square formats for Meta feed ad placements.

Software & Stack

Tools & Technical Craft

Post-production is performed using industry-standard tools — Adobe Premiere Pro, After Effects, and DaVinci Resolve for studio-grade color grading and clean audio mixing. Sound effects (SFX), background music balancing, and pacing are treated as critical narrative levers.

Psychology of Retention

Editing Built for Engagement, Not Just Polish

A visually stunning video that loses 70% of viewers in the first 3 seconds has failed. We apply direct-response hook architecture, dynamic zooms, contextual B-roll, and typographic text highlights to continuously reset viewer attention and maximize average watch time.
