What a Digital Marketing Consultant Actually Does

A digital marketing consultant's job is different: full-funnel digital marketing advisory that connects digital media planning & buying, inbound and outbound marketing strategy, and execution into one plan that's accountable to a number - leads, sales, or revenue - rather than impressions.
