This is different from running paid social ads: SMO is the ongoing work of optimizing profiles, content, and engagement so a brand's social presence builds real, organic momentum over time.

SMO is the foundational, largely organic work &#8211; optimizing profiles, bios, visual consistency, hashtag strategy, and posting patterns so a brand&#8217;s social presence is discoverable and credible on its own.

SMM more often refers to the broader marketing activity built on top of that foundation, including paid promotion and campaign-driven content.

In practice, SMO is what SMM performs better on. A well-optimized profile with strong organic engagement makes every paid campaign more effective, because the audience landing on the profile sees a consistent, trustworthy brand rather than an inconsistent feed.

Social signals aren&#8217;t a direct Google ranking factor the way backlinks or technical SEO are, but SMO supports SEO in a few concrete ways: optimized social profiles rank in search results for brand name searches, consistent cross-platform branding reinforces brand trust and authority building (which ties into E-E-A-T signals), and social media traffic to a website is a real, trackable channel that reduces dependence on organic search alone. A brand with strong organic social presence also tends to get referenced and linked more, which indirectly supports SEO over time.
