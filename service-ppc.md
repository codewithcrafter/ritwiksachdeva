### Frequently Asked Questions

#### Who is the best PPC expert or Google Ads consultant in Delhi?
It depends on your account type and goals — e-commerce Shopping campaigns, B2B lead generation, or local service-based search ads all require different expertise. The better filter is whether the consultant audits your account and asks about your actual CPA targets before proposing a plan.

#### How much does a Google Ads consultant charge per month in Delhi?
It depends on account complexity, number of campaigns, and whether CRO and landing page work is included. Management fees are typically separate from ad spend, and an accurate quote requires understanding your specific scope.

#### What is the difference between a PPC consultant and a Google Ads agency?
A consultant usually works directly with you with more flexibility for fast changes; an agency runs accounts through a larger team, which can suit bigger, more complex accounts. The right choice depends on account size and how hands-on you want the relationship to be.

#### What is the minimum ad budget needed for Google Ads in Delhi?
There's no universal minimum; it depends on your industry's CPC, competitiveness, and campaign goal. The practical approach is estimating what you can afford to spend per conversion (target CPA) based on your average deal or order value, then sizing the budget to generate enough data to optimize against.

#### What services does a PPC management engagement include?
Typically account audits, campaign setup and management across Search, Shopping, Display, and YouTube, conversion tracking setup (GTM/GA4), bid strategy management, ad copywriting and testing, and CRO or landing page recommendations.

#### How do I choose the right Google Ads consultant for my business?
Look for someone who starts with an account audit rather than jumping straight to recommendations, is transparent about realistic CPA and timeline expectations, and is willing to work within your target numbers.

#### Do you manage Google Shopping and Performance Max (PMax) campaigns?
Yes — Shopping and PMax campaign management is part of the service, with attention to product feed quality, negative keywords, audience signals, and target ROAS optimization.

#### Can you fix an underperforming Google Ads account that is wasting budget?
Yes, this usually starts with an account audit to find where the budget is being wasted (broad match bleed, poor landing pages, untracked conversions) and restructuring from there.

#### Does Google Ads certification guarantee good campaign results?
It's a reasonable signal of platform knowledge but not a guarantee of results on its own. It's worth checking whether the consultant has hands-on experience managing accounts at your scale and understands the business side of CPA and ROAS, not just the technical settings.

#### Do you manage all Google Ads formats including Search, Display, and YouTube?
Yes — the service covers Search, Shopping/PMax, Display remarketing, and YouTube video ads, since the most effective campaigns often use these formats together.
