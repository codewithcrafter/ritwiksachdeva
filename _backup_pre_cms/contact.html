Common Questions About Engagements.

Direct answers to common questions asked by prospective clients and executive teams.

Traditional marketing agencies typically experience high employee turnover and frequently delegate accounts to junior staff once contracts are signed. When you work with me, you collaborate directly with an experienced marketing consultant who has managed multi-million-dollar ad spend, engineered organic search strategies, and understands P&L economics. Every decision is tied to commercial business outcomes rather than agency billable hours.

Consulting engagements generally begin with a focused 90-day diagnostic sprint or strategic roadmap. This timeframe allows sufficient time to audit historical performance, optimize tracking, test new creative approaches, and demonstrate measurable impact. Following this initial period, clients often continue on a monthly advisory retainer or transition to internal execution with full documentation.

Paid advertising adjustments and conversion rate optimization improvements typically show early indicators (reduced cost-per-click, improved lead quality) within the first 14 to 30 days. Organic search, brand positioning, and content marketing architectures compound over 60 to 120 days. Rather than making exaggerated promises, we focus on disciplined hypothesis testing with measurable weekly progress.
