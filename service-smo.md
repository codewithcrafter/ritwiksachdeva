### Frequently Asked Questions

#### What is included in SMO (Social Media Optimization) services?
Typically a social media audit, profile and bio optimization across platforms, banner and profile asset design, Story Highlights setup, hashtag and keyword strategy, internal link setup, and brand consistency guidelines.

#### How much do SMO services cost in Delhi?
It depends on the number of platforms, whether content creation is included, and whether it is a one-time optimization project or an ongoing monthly retainer. A proposal is customized to your specific channels.

#### What is the difference between SMO and SMM?
SMO is the organic, foundational work of optimizing profiles, branding, and content structure. SMM refers to broader marketing, especially paid advertising campaigns (Meta Ads, LinkedIn Ads) to reach new audiences.

#### How does social media optimization help with SEO?
Optimized profiles often rank on page one of Google for brand name searches, drive referral traffic, and generate social signals that reinforce overall brand authority and search presence.

#### Which social media platforms do you optimize?
We optimize Instagram, LinkedIn, YouTube, Facebook, Pinterest, and Twitter / X, tailoring profile architecture and search keywords to the unique audience of each channel.

#### Do SMO services include creating and posting content?
Standard SMO focuses on profile structure, branding, bio, and templates. Content creation, graphic design, and ongoing publishing can be bundled into a comprehensive social media management engagement.

#### How long does a social media optimization project take?
A one-time complete profile overhaul across 3 to 4 platforms typically takes 7 to 10 business days from the initial audit to final banner and asset deployment.

#### Why is SMO important before running paid social ads?
When visitors see an ad, many click through to the brand profile to verify legitimacy. If the profile has low-quality visuals, broken links, or an unclear bio, they leave without converting, wasting your ad budget.
