## Frequently Asked Questions (Comprehensive Q&A)

### Which is the best product photography studio in Delhi?

The best choice depends on what you are shooting  catalog shots for Amazon have different requirements than high-fashion lifestyle shoots with models. Ritwik Sachdeva provides commercial product photography tailored specifically to e-commerce conversion, combining technical studio quality with marketing strategy.

### How much does a commercial product photoshoot cost in Delhi?

Pricing is typically structured per SKU (product), per image, or as a day-rate for studio sessions. Costs vary based on whether the shoot requires basic white background, complex prop styling, or live models.

### What is included in a complete product photoshoot package?

A standard package includes studio space and lighting, camera equipment, shoot execution per approved shot list, basic-to-advanced retouching (color grading, background cleanup, clipping paths), and delivery in multiple resolutions.

### Do you provide props and model casting for product shoots?

Yes. For styled flat lays and lifestyle shoots, we curate appropriate background textures, lifestyle props, and coordinate model casting and hair/makeup artists tailored to your brand aesthetic.

### What is the difference between tabletop, flat lay, and lifestyle product shoots?

Tabletop photography captures products at eye-level on clean surfaces; flat lay captures products arranged overhead with thematic props; lifestyle photography captures products in realistic context being used by models.

### Can you shoot pure white background images compliant with Amazon guidelines?

Yes. We shoot on pure RGB 255, 255, 255 white backgrounds meeting Amazon, Flipkart, and international marketplace standards, with product filling 85%+ of the frame without artificial watermarks.

### Do you offer on-site shoots at factories or warehouses?

Yes. For manufacturers and industrial clients in Okhla, Naraina, or Greater Noida with bulky products or massive SKU counts, we bring portable studio lighting and backdrops directly to your facility.

### How many edited high-resolution images are provided per SKU?

A typical e-commerce package delivers 4 to 7 edited angles per SKU: hero front shot, 45-degree angle, back/side view, close-up texture detail, and dimension/scale context.

### Can you design Amazon A+ Content and EBC banners?

Yes. Beyond capturing raw photography, we provide graphic design services to composite product images into high-converting Amazon A+ Content, brand store headers, and infographics.

### What is the typical delivery turnaround for product photography?

Standard delivery is 3 to 5 business days after shoot completion. Urgent 48-hour turnarounds can be accommodated for critical catalog launches.
