01 / DISCOVERY

What a Digital Marketing Consultant Actually Does

A lot of businesses hire "digital marketing help" and end up with someone running ads or posting content in isolation, with no connective strategy behind either. A digital marketing consultant's job is different: full-funnel digital marketing advisory that connects digital media planning & buying, inbound and outbound marketing strategy, and execution into one plan that's accountable to a number - leads, sales, or revenue - rather than impressions.

As a digital marketing strategist in Delhi, Ritwik works across strategy and execution together, which matters because a channel plan that ignores strategy usually burns budget, and a strategy that never gets executed properly never shows results either.

04 / SELECTION CRITERIA

Choosing a Digital Marketing Consultant in Delhi

For small businesses and startups, the right digital marketing consultant in Delhi is usually the one willing to start with your numbers - current CAC, conversion rate, average order value - rather than a generic package. A freelance digital marketing consultant working directly with you (as opposed to being routed through account managers at a large agency) tends to move faster on strategy changes, since there's no internal hand-off between the person deciding the strategy and the person adjusting the campaign.

On cost: digital marketing consultancy pricing in Delhi varies a lot depending on scope - a one-time strategy audit is priced very differently from ongoing full-funnel management across SEO, PPC, and social. Rather than quote a number that wouldn't apply to your specific situation, the more useful first step is usually a conversation about what you actually need done.

For startups specifically looking for an affordable digital marketing consultant, the practical approach is often to start with a focused engagement - one channel or one funnel stage - and expand once that's showing results, instead of committing to a full retainer upfront.
